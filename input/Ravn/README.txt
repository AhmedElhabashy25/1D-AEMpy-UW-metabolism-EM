


Each datafile will start with the unique lake ID number in the dataset, followed by a dash "-", then the data type identifier, and ends with the datafile number

e.g., for Lake RAVN

Hydrodynamics
L0001-HD01.csv 	==> Discharge at the inlet (m3/s)
L0001-HD02.csv	==> Discharge at the outlet (m3/s)

L0001-HD03.csv	==>	Temperature in-lake (degrees Celsius)
L0001-HD04.csv	==>	Dissolved Oxygen concentration and saturation in-lake (mg/L) & (%)
L0001-HD05.csv	==>	pH in-lake (-)

L0001-HD06.csv	==>	Hypsography: depth (m) & area (m2)



Water Quality Files
L0001-WQ01.csv	==> Phosphorus concentrations at inlet (mg-P/L)
L0001-WQ02.csv	==> Nitrogen concentrations at inlet (mg-N/L)
L0001-WQ03.csv	==> Phosphorus concentrations at outlet (mg-P/L)
L0001-WQ04.csv	==> Nitrogen concentrations at outlet (mg-N/L)

L0001-WQ05.csv 	==> Phosphorus concentrations in-lake (mg-P/L)
L0001-WQ06.csv 	==> Nitrogen concentrations in-lake (mg-N/L)
L0001-WQ07.csv 	==> Chlorophyll-a concentrations in-lake (µg/L)



Meteorological: ERA5 Reanalysis (hourly) [open-meteo.com]
L0001-MET01.csv ==> Precipitatoin over the lake (mm)
				==> Air temperature at 2m over the lake (degrees Celsius)
				==> Wind Speed and direction at 10m (m/s) & (degrees)
				==> Shortwave radiations (W/m2)
				==> Relative Humidity at 2m (%)
